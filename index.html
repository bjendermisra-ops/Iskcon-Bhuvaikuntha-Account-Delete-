What Data Will Be Deleted?
  • Account credentials & login session tokens
  • Devotee profile details (Name, address, Gotra, avatar)
  • Device notification tokens and preferences
  • Optional app usage records & preferences
Information Retained for Legal Compliance

As required by Indian Statutory Laws, certain records cannot be deleted immediately:

  • Donation transactions & Payment Reference IDs
  • Income Tax 80G & Form 10BE statutory records
  • Financial audit & regulatory accounting entries
  • Security/fraud detection logs
Retained data is securely archived solely for legal compliance and is never utilized for active accounts or marketing.
